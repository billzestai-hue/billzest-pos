Questions we hear often

How much does it cost?

Starter is free with 50 invoices a month. Business is SAR 251 a month or SAR 2,000 a year, with a 14-day free trial. New customers get 10% off the first payment with code WELCOME10.

Does it support ZATCA e-invoicing?

E-invoicing is part of the product. Message us and we will confirm which ZATCA phase applies to your business and how to set it up.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works on Android, iPhone and any computer browser with the same account.

Can I import my old data?

You can upload customers and items from Excel. Our team helps with your first import for free.

Is my data safe?

Your data is encrypted and backed up every day. You can export it whenever you want.

How many people can use it?

Business includes 3 users and Enterprise includes 10. Each user can sign in on a phone or a computer.

Do I need special equipment?

No. A phone or a computer is enough. A barcode scanner and a thermal printer make the counter faster, but they are optional.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Tell us before your next payment date and we will stop the plan. You can export your data first.
